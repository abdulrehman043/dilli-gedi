Use the on-screen buttons: arrows on the left steer, the arrows on the right move you forward and back. Tap In/out beside a parked vehicle to drive it. Drift is the handbrake, Reset puts you back on the nearest road.

Jobs start at the glowing markers around Connaught Place: green for auto fares, orange for parcels, pink for the outer circle race. Crash into traffic and the police will come looking.
